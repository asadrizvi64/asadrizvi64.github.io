STAGE 01 · ABOUT

PLAYER PROFILE

I'm an AI engineer based in Dresden, Germany, working across three layers of the stack: perception (segmentation, feature matching, reconstruction), generation (diffusion, GANs, LoRA fine-tuning) and language (LLM fine-tuning, RAG, agent runtimes).

At Imarat Group (2024–2026) I built generative tools for architecture: floorplan-to-render pipelines, Stable Diffusion + ControlNet fine-tunes and city-planning research, and cut load times on our AI systems by 97%. As a forward-deployed AI engineer at SynthralOS I co-architected a multi-tenant operating system for AI agents. Through KhyaalTech I ship vision, 3D and generative systems for client teams.

I studied AI at FAST-NUCES (BS, 2023). My final-year project, KisaanDost, put a fine-tuned Bloomz model behind an Urdu voice interface for farmers. Most of my day is Python: training loops, data pipelines, and the infrastructure that gets models into production.

Favourite kind of problem: the research isn't finished, but the ship date is already set.
